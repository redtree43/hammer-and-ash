BUILD 09.1 — SENTINEL BREACH AND COVER SPECIALISATION
Compared with 09:
- Razor Area Shred destroys light cover only, leaving reinforced intact.
- Sentinel Breach is a cardinal straight-line 1–4 tile charge (2 AP),
  breaking authored destructible light and reinforced cover on traversed
  edges and dealing 5 impact damage on enemy collision.
- Added weight-checked, deterministic one-tile enemy displacement with
  invalid heavy/blocked destinations shown before committing AP.
- Added a shared Breach plan for accurate previews and execution, with
  blue valid paths, orange destruction markers, green pushes and red X.
- Breach resolves tile by tile with existing reactions and hazard rules.
- Breach now contributes damage, distance and ability use to existing
  hit ledger and per-room activity report.
- Existing three-room mission, radio consequence, room-jump shortcuts,
  health persistence, armour refresh, enemy AI and other environment
  mechanics retained unchanged.
