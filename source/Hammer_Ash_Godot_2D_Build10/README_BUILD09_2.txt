HAMMER & ASH - BUILD 09.2
Import project.godot in Godot 4 and press F5.

RIGHT SIDEBAR:
- Choose one of the five small squad tabs, or press 1-5/Tab.
- Selected operative card shows stats, weight, status and abilities.
- Hover an enemy to display that enemy's stats, active effects and attack preview.
- The objective card shows the radio state in Room 2.

ENVIRONMENT:
- One fuel and one waste barrel in each room, not next to each other.
- Breakable cover is 2 structural HP (light), 5 (reinforced); hidden from player UI.
- Already Burning/Irradiated/Shocked: moving onto the same hazard type does not cause another entry hit or refresh the status; timed effects continue.

TEST QUICKLY: F6 / F7 / F8 jump to a fresh squad in the corresponding room.
The full mission carries squad health and injuries between rooms.
