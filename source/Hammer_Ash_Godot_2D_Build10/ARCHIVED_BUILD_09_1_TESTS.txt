HAMMER & ASH: UPRISING — BUILD 09.1 TEST PLAN

HOW TO RUN
1. Extract the ZIP to a fresh folder.
2. Import its project.godot in Godot 4.x and press F5.
3. In the game use F6/F7/F8 (or visible room buttons) to jump to rooms
   for individual tests. Those developer jumps start with a fresh squad.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE BUILD 09

RAZOR AREA SHRED [Q]
- Charge up to three pathfinding steps and sweep three connected adjacent cells.
- Deals 2 damage to affected enemies as before.
- Damages ONLY light (1-cover-level / 2-structural-HP) cover sections.
- Does NOT damage reinforced (2-cover-level / 5-structural-HP) cover.
- Fuel/waste explosions still affect BOTH light and reinforced cover.

SENTINEL BREACH [B] — PROVISIONAL TEST ABILITY
- Select Sentinel (key 2), then press B to enter Breach targeting.
- Hover a destination 1-4 tiles away N/E/S/W. No diagonal paths or turns.
- Blue outline: valid landing tile. Orange ring: cover section destroyed.
- Red X: invalid target; read the ATTACK/AREA PREVIEW panel for why.
- Green outlined tile/line: where a living lighter target will be pushed.
- 5 DMG label: raw damage on collision, normally absorbed by armour first.
- Click a valid destination. Costs 2 AP total and one use each player turn.
- Move one tile at a time, breaking authored light/reinforced cover crossed.
- An enemy on the chosen destination is struck for 5 raw damage. If it
  survives and its weight is below Sentinel's, it is displaced ONE tile.
- Displacement preference: straight ahead; then side; then other side.
- Sentinel enters the enemy's former tile. If enemy dies, it no longer
  occupies the tile and no displacement is needed.
- If enemy weight >= Sentinel's, the landing tile is INVALID before AP cost.
- If an enemy survives and no displacement tile is free, target INVALID.
- An ally, another enemy along the intermediate route, a wall, barrel,
  transmitter, or unbreakable fixed cover invalidates the attempted path.
- Overwatch may interrupt the moving Sentinel. Hazards affect Sentinel
  and any knocked-back victim under existing status and damage rules.
- Fortify [Q] remains unchanged. Breach does NOT replace Fortify.
- Breach movement/damage/cover broken are recorded in squad activity and
  the hit ledger [L], where the effect is listed as Sentinel Breach.

IMPORTANT DESIGN NOTE
The prototype has authored destructible cover sections with small WHITE HP
markers. These can be destroyed by Sentinel Breach and explosive attacks.
Other cover sections are fixed, so Breach cannot pass through those edges.
The mark is the distinction. Ordinary shots cannot damage cover.
