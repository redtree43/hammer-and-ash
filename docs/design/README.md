## How to use this specification

- **Confirmed rule** means the documented commitment governs implementation. **Confirmed direction** fixes the goal or approach while leaving details open. The headings and labels in each owner document identify the applicable status.
- **Open** means creator review is needed before choosing a permanent rule. Prototype settings can continue to run as experiments. [Development Status](../DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md) describes current behavior and gaps. [Build 10 playtests](../BUILD_10_PLAYTEST.md) record verification.
- When the creator makes a new decision, update the owning document, record its date and source, and close or revise the related review item. A new creator decision takes precedence and becomes part of the specification through that update.
- If sources disagree or approval is unclear, record the conflict in [Open decisions](OPEN_DECISIONS.md) before setting a permanent default. The retained handoffs in the parent folder, the campaign transfer package, and the original references provide provenance and prototype context.
