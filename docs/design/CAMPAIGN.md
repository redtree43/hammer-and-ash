## Confirmed campaign structure

- The campaign combines strategic freedom with authored milestones and a partially randomised offer structure. The target is 15–30 hours, with longer runs possible on higher difficulties. Keep strategic management compact and focused on tactical preparation.
- Use a stylised map of eight federal regions and authored tactical locations. The Central Federal District hosts the Moscow finale and is excluded from regional liberation and lockout counts. The other seven districts are eligible. Offer two randomly selected eligible districts when at least two are available. A passed-over district that is still eligible returns to the pool with the same chance as any other eligible district. When only one district is eligible, offer it immediately so the player can commit. The player stays committed until liberation or permanent loss. The creator confirmed the single-offer and fresh-draw rules on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Show campaign days for immersion. Passage of days alone leaves the story and game state unchanged. Campaign progression, including changes to operation availability, comes from specified outcomes or events. Offer limited operations with some mutually exclusive or expiring opportunities. The creator confirmed the calendar’s role on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- At campaign milestones, use both authored strategic event triggers and random draws from eligible events. The creator confirmed both selection methods on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. The event catalogue, eligibility and frequency remain open.
- A mission can advance regional liberation only if its lead objective succeeds. Other gains can still carry forward under the [secured reward rules](MISSIONS.md#secured-rewards). Liberation progress eventually unlocks a decisive operation. Defeating the regional commander is required for liberation. Boss variants are confirmed direction. Exact variations remain open. The creator confirmed the lead-objective requirement on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Regional bosses can take authored strategic actions before the liberation operation, such as reinforcing a facility or changing which operations are offered. The creator confirmed this scope on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. Each boss’s actions and triggers remain open.
- A visible regional pressure measure can permanently lock a region, including an unchosen district before the player commits to it. Local pressure increases only after specified events or outcomes. Pressure already gained stays in place. Successful outcomes can prevent further increases and advance liberation where applicable. Liberated regions stay secure and grant network access. The approved reward direction is a chosen permanent specialisation and a smaller immediate prize. The creator confirmed these lockout and local-pressure rules on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Losing a region preserves secured mission rewards. A lockout ends access to contacts and research projects that still depend on that district’s network. After losing a region the player committed to, if the campaign can continue, offer one HQ decision that provides limited help, such as emergency supplies or faster treatment. The available help, amounts and timing remain open. The creator confirmed the network access and HQ recovery rules on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. Balance campaign rewards and losses to preserve meaningful tradeoffs and room to recover.

## Victory, defeat and Soviet response

- Liberating four of the seven eligible districts unlocks the Moscow finale. All lockouts among those seven districts count toward the campaign defeat threshold, including districts locked out before the player committed to them.
- The Moscow finale is a sequence of operations that leads to the Politburo confrontation. Each operation has its own objectives and deployment. Failed operations can be retried while the campaign can continue, including after the first defeat by the Politburo. Losses carry forward and the displayed campaign date retains the time spent. Operation order and objectives remain open. [Moscow review items](OPEN_DECISIONS.md#campaign-and-headquarters)
- The campaign ends in defeat only when four eligible districts are locked out or the player loses to the Politburo a second time. Each unsuccessful deployment into the Politburo confrontation counts as one loss, including voluntary extraction before completing it. The creator confirmed both defeat conditions and the extraction rule on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- The Soviet escalation meter changes only after specified outcomes, such as district liberations and authored mission results. Elapsed campaign days leave the meter unchanged. Each district liberation increases the meter by the same amount. Soviet escalation stages are permanent once reached. Missions can delay the next stage, and the current stage’s effects stay in force. Global escalation stages can change both campaign conditions and tactical conditions in newly deployed missions. For example, a stage may put selected facilities on higher alert before deployment. The size of the increase and the effects of each stage remain open.
- At maximum escalation, the meter caps and the final stage stays in force. Active programme modifiers follow their own end conditions. Subsequent district events use their usual pressure rules. The creator confirmed the endpoint on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Soviet programmes activate only when the escalation meter crosses a designated threshold. A threshold can activate multiple eligible programmes together. Specific mission outcomes and district liberations determine eligibility. Draw at random from the eligible programmes when resolving a threshold. If one increase crosses several designated thresholds, resolve each one in ascending order, from lowest to highest. Each programme can activate only once per campaign and leaves the eligible pool after activation. The creator confirmed the random draw, resolution order and one-activation limit on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. Activation limits for individual thresholds remain open. [Programme review](OPEN_DECISIONS.md#escalation-and-soviet-programmes)
- The current design direction is for a Soviet programme to apply a campaign, enemy or mission modifier when it activates. Set an end condition per modifier. It can last for the rest of the campaign, expire or end when a specified objective is completed. The creator confirmed the per-modifier rule on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Tactical adaptation uses observed player behaviour to introduce a small number of soft counters, typically one or two, in following regions. Keep adaptation distinct from Soviet strategic programmes and make the consequences of both readable.

## Headquarters and personnel

- Use a compact, visually immersive HQ currently envisioned as an underground bunker. Keep its functions independent of the visual theme. Facility improvements should be limited and meaningful.
- The Personnel dossier shows a large operative portrait on the left, the class path in the centre, and the equipped Supersuit and consumable above the operative’s current stats on the right. Stats include applicable bonuses and penalties from the operative’s equipment and selected class abilities. Use distinct shapes for active and passive nodes, grey out unselected and locked abilities, and show each ability’s details on hover. The creator confirmed the layout and hover behaviour on 26 November 2026 in the Personnel screen request and attached sketch.
- Choose five ready operatives from one shared roster for each mission when at least five are ready. When only one to four are ready, the player can deploy a short squad and advance recovery for wounded operatives at HQ. The creator confirmed short-squad deployment on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. There are no fixed squads or saved presets.
- Mission rewards and narrative events can add recruits. The base provides a reliable recruitment path and lets the player choose each recruit’s class. HQ recruits are ready to deploy immediately once the player meets the recruitment requirements and commits the required resources. The creator confirmed immediate recruitment on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. Costs and recruitment limits remain open.
- If no operative survives and the player cannot afford ordinary recruitment, HQ provides one ready emergency recruit immediately while the campaign is still active. The creator confirmed this fallback on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Serious injury and permanent loss make a deep roster important. A wounded operative recovers at HQ while sitting out operations. Completing operations advances recovery for wounded operatives left at HQ. Which outcomes count and how much recovery is needed remain open. Tactical healing and downing are defined in [Combat](COMBAT.md#downing-and-recovery).
- **Skip Operation** advances recovery without deploying a squad, including when nobody is ready. The skipped operation defines its own consequences. Some skips can raise regional pressure and contribute to losing the region. Others can simply forfeit the opportunity. The creator confirmed the option and its per-operation consequences on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. Other effects of skipping remain open. [Open decisions](OPEN_DECISIONS.md#campaign-and-headquarters)

## Economy, Workshop and research

- **Supplies** and **Intel** are the two main spendable currencies. The Workshop uses Supplies to manufacture consumables only.
- Players can obtain [Supersuit variants](COMBAT.md#equipment) through mission and event rewards or through research unlocks and HQ requisition. The creator confirmed both routes on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review. The reward catalogue and requisition costs remain open.
- When an operative is permanently lost, the squad must secure their Supersuit in the field to retain it. Use the operation’s [stated securing condition](MISSIONS.md#secured-rewards). Clearing the room where the operative fell is one possible condition. The creator confirmed field recovery on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Research uses one visible internal tree with three branches. **Workshop** unlocks consumable recipes. **Stratagems** unlock in-mission support. **Intel Ops** unlock strategic information and mission preparation options. Regional networks, discoveries and secured objectives can gate research.
- A research project unlocks immediately when the player meets its prerequisites and commits the required resources. The creator confirmed immediate unlocks on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Put Workshop recipe research on the **Research** screen alongside Stratagems and Intel Ops. Manufacture unlocked consumables on the **Workshop** screen. The creator confirmed this split on 25 November 2026 in the Open Decisions review.
- Intel pays for eligible preparation and tactical support. The player can commit some support before deployment and spend Intel on approved actions during combat. Research should broaden options and decisions with limited passive stat growth.
- Show rewards, active Soviet programme effects, research prerequisites, recruiting completion, Intel costs and lockout warnings where they inform decisions.

### Operative XP and earned levels

**27 November 2026, creator's approval of the enemy tier and levelling plan in chat.** Pool tier XP from defeated enemies with objective XP across the operation. Each surviving deployed operative receives the full pool at successful debrief or on controlled extraction from cleared rooms. The existing Personnel trees unlock at the earned level. Manual level selection remains available for development.

**Trial tuning.** New operatives start at Level 1. Level 2 requires 18 cumulative XP and Level 3 requires 45. Each completed generator or prisoner objective pays 4 XP. Failed operations, the regression fixture and deployments using a manual level pay zero campaign XP. Downed operatives who survive receive the same XP as other survivors. The trial caps earned levels at 3 and continues tracking total XP.
