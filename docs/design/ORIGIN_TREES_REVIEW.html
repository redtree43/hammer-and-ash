HAMMER & ASH / DESIGN REVIEW / UPDATED 26 NOV 2026

Eleven origin trees

This sheet preserves the 26 November review. The 27 November class update, Iskra redesign and Glaz and Alkhimik update document the changes now shown in Build 10 Personnel.

Choose an origin to review its nine levels. Jaguar is the reference sheet. Each choice is independent; choosing the left node never locks you into the left column at a later level. The Scorpion, Anvil, Drone Command and Healing trees reflect the creator's 26 November direction. The Drone Command and Healing builds are proposals. Items marked for review still need a decision.

The combat spec uses one untyped damage value. The marked protection and damage bonuses need review under that rule.

Reading the trees

Level 1 grants both a shared class active and an origin passive, including any actions that passive unlocks. Levels 4 and 7 are shared by both origins. At levels 2, 3, 5, 6 and 9 choose one of the two nodes. Level 8 is automatic. Basic attacks and standard moves cost 1 AP. CD2 means the ability becomes available at the start of your second subsequent turn. Rooms reset cooldowns and encounter resources.

Range uses grid tiles including diagonals. Ranged powers require line of sight and respect directional cover unless specified. Area attacks affect allies unless they explicitly select foes. Count each unit once per area. Flight and hops require a legal landing, cannot cross solid walls or ceilings, and trigger landing hazards. Forced movement respects weight, footprint and legal destinations. Glaz’s voluntary ally slides are usable on otherwise empty clear tiles; allied movement still resolves landing hazards.

Temporary armour absorbs before ordinary armour, expires as specified and never raises permanent capacity. Healing respects downing and wound limits. Armour piercing bypasses current armour entirely. Heat may never exceed four. Charged ranged basics provisionally hold three charges and recharge for 1 AP. Separate powers spend the listed AP, charges or heat.

Drone orders

Drone Command deploys one drone alongside the operative, with no AP cost. The drone occupies a tile, can be targeted and has no independent AP. A standard order costs the operator 1 AP and uses the drone's activation for the turn. The operator can issue orders from anywhere in the room. A standard order lets the drone move up to two tiles, fire Arc Bolt, or use Rolling Barricade. Chain Arc instead costs 2 AP and uses the same activation. Measure attack range and line of sight from the drone. Suit and repair abilities use their own listed targeting rules. The drone follows its operator to each linked room. It keeps its HP and follows the normal room-entry armour refresh. A destroyed drone stays lost for the rest of the operation unless Emergency Rebuild restores it.

First playtest

Use the same team and substitute the two origins of one class. Try the fleeing officer, hounds protecting a healer, and the prototype robot. Record who reaches the target by turn two, AP spent on setup, HP lost in the enemy reply and what the weaker matchup can still accomplish. For Scorpion compare Jaguar on the same map.

Scorpion

Catch a runner and decide whether to keep both fighters pinned or let go and finish it with venom.

Melee · Tail Sting, 2 damage
01Gain both
Charge Spend 1–3 AP. Gain two movement tiles per AP spent for this turn. No built-in attack. CD2.
Segmented Carapace +1 armour capacity, standard move four. Direct Tail Sting hits, including basic hits granted by skills, give organic foes Venom: 1 damage at each of their next two phase starts.
02Passive · pick 1
Deflective Plates Gain +1 armour capacity.
Stronger Poison Your Venom lasts one additional tick.
03Active · pick 1
Claw Grab 1 AP · CD2. Hit an adjacent foe for 2 damage. Both units lose voluntary movement until your next turn. Either unit being displaced or downed ends the hold.
Hooked Pincers 1 AP · CD2. Pull one eligible foe from two tiles away one tile toward you. If it lands adjacent, strike for 2 damage.
04Class passive
Threatening Presence Reaction · once per round · Make a basic melee hit when an adjacent foe walks away. Shoves, leaps and teleports skip this reaction.
05Active · pick 1
Carapace Slam 2 AP · CD2. Hit one adjacent foe for 2 damage plus your current armour, capped at +3.
Venom Jet 1 AP · CD2. Spray a straight three-tile line. Give each organic unit Venom with zero impact damage. Solid walls stop the spray.
06Passive · pick 1
Moulting Skin Once per room, after an incoming hit depletes your current armour, resolve any HP spillover first. If still standing, immediately refill armour to its current maximum.
Barbed Retort Once per round, after surviving a direct adjacent melee hit, answer with Tail Sting if the attacker is still adjacent.
07Class active
Breach Relay 1 AP · once per room · Hit an adjacent foe, then hold position, step one clear tile, or swap places with an adjacent squadmate.
08Origin passive
Numbing Sting A unit carrying your Venom moves one fewer tile per standard move, minimum one.
09Capstone · pick 1
Burrow Defense Once per operation, when an attack would down you, reduce your HP to 1 and burrow underground. You are immune to damage for the rest of the round. At the start of the next player turn, emerge at 1 HP and clear all status effects.
Burrowing Strike 2 AP · once per room. Tunnel up to four tiles beneath units and low cover to a visible empty tile at your elevation, then Tail Sting an adjacent foe. Cannot pass walls or trigger walking reactions.

Example turn Spend AP on Charge to reach a fleeing officer, then use Claw Grab if you have AP left.

Under pressure Machines ignore Venom. Your pincers and body remain effective, but distant shooters can punish a prolonged hold.

Drone Command

Set up an arc that jumps through a squad or drive a portable barricade into open ground. Your light suit helps the drone shape the fight and lets you keep contributing if it goes down.

Ranged · Arc Pistol, 2 damage, reach four, charged
01Gain both
Induction Tap 1 AP · CD2. Pulse one visible foe within three for 1 damage. A robot also loses two current armour before the hit.
Paired Deployment Deploy with one drone on an adjacent legal tile. Your light power armour grants +1 armour capacity. The drone has 4 HP, 1 armour capacity, and a 2-damage Arc Bolt at reach four. See Drone orders above.
02Passive · pick 1
Assault Chassis Unlock Chain Arc as a 2 AP drone order, CD2. Hit a visible foe within four tiles for 3 damage, then choose up to two more foes for 2 and 1 damage. Each jump can bend around a corner if the next foe is within two tiles of, and visible from, the previous target. A unit can only be hit once per arc.
Guardian Chassis Gain +1 drone armour capacity and unlock Rolling Barricade as a drone order, CD2. Move the drone up to three legal tiles and choose a cardinal facing. It projects three contiguous full-cover edges perpendicular to that facing, centred on its forward edge. Preview the line before committing. The line provides cover without blocking movement and lasts until your next turn or until the drone moves or is destroyed.
03Active · pick 1
Magnetic Tow 1 AP · CD2. Pull a visible standard-or-lighter enemy within three tiles of the drone one legal tile toward it. Measure visibility from the drone. This leaves the drone's activation available, so you can pull an enemy into an arc or out from behind cover before giving the order.
Field Service 1 AP · once per room. Service the adjacent drone to restore 2 HP and reset its chassis signature cooldown. This leaves its activation available and requires a surviving drone.
04Class passive
Grounded Chassis If you end your turn without moving, reduce damage from the first incoming direct hit by 1 before your next turn. Moving ends the protection.
05Active · pick 1
Rescue Winch 1 AP · CD2. Swap the drone with a visible, willing one-tile squadmate within three tiles of the drone. Both destinations must be legal. Resolve landing hazards. This uses the drone's activation and gives the rescued ally two temporary armour until your next turn.
Shock Mine 1 AP · CD2. Place a visible mine on an empty tile within two tiles and line of sight of either the operator or the drone. The first enemy to step onto it before your next turn takes 2 damage and becomes Shocked. Only one mine can be active. The operator can place it even after the drone is destroyed.
06Passive · pick 1
Crossfire Once per turn, your first Arc Pistol hit that comes from a different cover-facing side than the drone's earlier hit on the same target this turn deals +2 damage. For a Chain Arc jump, use the tile of the previous target as the source.
Retaliation Circuit Once per round, after surviving a direct hit from an enemy it can legally target with Arc Bolt, the drone returns fire. This reaction uses neither operator AP nor the drone's activation.
07Class active
Field Rewire 1 AP · once per room. Restore up to two current armour to yourself or a visible allied machine within three, including your drone. This does not repair HP or rebuild a destroyed drone.
08Origin passive
Tandem Drive Once per turn, after the operator takes a standard move, the drone may move up to two legal tiles without spending its activation. The drone can take this move anywhere in the room.
09Capstone · pick 1
Emergency Rebuild 2 AP · once per operation. If the drone was destroyed, rebuild it with its learned upgrades on a legal adjacent tile at half maximum HP, rounded up. It can activate on your next turn.
Overclock Link 2 AP · once per room. Spend the drone's unspent activation to use its chassis signature even if on cooldown, then set its normal CD2. Assault may move up to three legal tiles first and arc through up to four foes for 4, 3, 2 and 1 damage. Guardian uses Rolling Barricade and can project a second line on a different side of the drone. Up to two standing squadmates adjacent to the drone also gain two temporary armour until your next turn.

Assault turn Pull a foe out from behind cover with Magnetic Tow, then spend the remaining 2 AP on Chain Arc with that foe as the first link.

Guardian turn Drive the drone ahead of the squad and put up Rolling Barricade. A Shock Mine can punish a flank beside the line. If you picked Rescue Winch instead, you can use it on a later turn to swap the drone with an ally caught out of position.

After a loss Arc Pistol, Induction Tap, Shock Mine if selected, and Field Rewire on yourself remain available.

Darwinian

Hop a gap or drag an enemy out of position as Frog, hold a choke as Jellyfish, or attack from range and foul an enemy's route as Skunk. Shift when the fight calls for a different tool.

Variable · Frog and Jellyfish melee, Skunk ranged
01Gain both
Field Formula 1 AP · CD2. Treat yourself or an adjacent standing squadmate for 2 HP, or deal 2 damage to an adjacent enemy and reduce its next direct hit before your next turn by 1 damage, to a minimum of 1. Both options are available in every form. Healing respects the wound ceiling.
Darwinian Shift Choose a starting form at deployment and retain your active form between rooms. Shift for 1 AP, once per turn. Each signature costs 1 AP and has its own CD2. Shifting preserves all cooldowns and does not consume HP or a separate resource.
  • Frog has +1 standard move and a 2-damage melee Bite. Tongue Vault hops up to three tiles to a legal landing, then may pull one visible standard-or-lighter enemy within two tiles one legal tile toward Frog.

02Passive · pick 1
Quick Adaptation After shifting, you may take one legal step in your new form. Resolve ordinary hazards and movement reactions.
Settled Form If you end your turn in the same form you started it in without shifting, reduce the first incoming direct hit by 1 before your next turn.
03Active · pick 1
Shed Skin 1 AP · CD2. Clear one of Burning, Venom or Blinded from yourself and shift. Counts as the turn's shift.
Rapid Metamorph 1 AP · CD2. Shift and immediately use the new form's signature if it is ready, for the total cost of 1 AP. This spends the turn's shift and puts the used signature on cooldown.
04Class passive
Clinical Eye Once per turn, add +1 HP to the first heal on a target below half its healing ceiling, or +1 damage to a Field Formula attack on a target below half maximum HP. The first qualifying action uses the bonus.
05Active · pick 1
Express Trait 2 AP · CD2 shared across all three variants. Frog hops up to three tiles to a legal landing, then strikes an adjacent foe for 2 damage, knocks it up to two tiles if eligible, and cancels its reactions until its next turn. Jellyfish stings an adjacent foe for 1 damage and, if it is standard-weight or lighter and eligible for Stun, disables its actions and reactions through the end of its next turn. This operative can inflict this stun once per room in total, including through Perfect Adaptation. Skunk sprays three consecutive visible tiles in a straight line within five for 3 damage to each occupying unit, including allies.
Passing Trait 1 AP · CD2. Choose a visible standing squadmate within three. Frog lets them take up to two legal steps immediately, resolving ordinary hazards and reactions without spending their AP. Jellyfish grants two temporary armour and reduces the first forced move against them by one tile before your next turn. Skunk adds two tiles of reach to the ally's next ranged basic attack this turn.
06Passive · pick 1
Vestigial Plates After you shift, gain two temporary armour until your next turn.
Form Mastery If you start your turn in the form you used for all of your previous turn, improve that form until you shift or the turn ends. Frog's Tongue Vault hop gains one tile, Jellyfish's Nettle Lock gains one damage, or Skunk's basic Spray gains one tile of reach.
07Class active
Reserve Dose 1 AP · once per room. Choose yourself or an adjacent standing ally. Clear Burning or Venom if present and add +1 damage to that unit’s next basic attack this turn. Usable in any form.
08Origin passive
Reflex Shift Once per room, take a standard shift for 0 AP. It still uses the turn's shift.
09Capstone · pick 1
Mosaic Body 2 AP · once per room. Use two different ready signatures in either order, temporarily borrowing their traits while keeping your active form. Both signatures enter their normal cooldowns. This does not use the turn's shift.
Perfect Adaptation 2 AP · once per room. Shift and immediately use the new form's Express Trait, even if you did not learn it or it is on cooldown. Counts as the turn's shift and starts Express Trait's normal cooldown if learned.

When to switch Use Frog when reach or displacement decides the fight. Stay Jellyfish when one enemy needs to be kept in a choke. Shift to Skunk when closing is unsafe or enemies must cross a lane you can coat.

Healing (Jekyll / Hyde)

Move wounded allies out of danger and clear space for the squad as Jekyll. Become Hyde to break a position or chase down a kill, then decide whether to take a second attack turn or return early to treat the squad.

Jekyll · Irritant Dart, 1 damage, reach four, uncharged / Hyde · Rending Strike, 3 melee damage
01Gain both
Field Formula 1 AP · CD2. Treat yourself or an adjacent standing squadmate for 2 HP, or deal 2 damage to an adjacent enemy and reduce its next direct hit before your next turn by 1 damage, to a minimum of 1. Both options are available in every form. Healing respects the wound ceiling.
Dual Nature Trial proposal. Start each room as Jekyll. Once per room, spend 1 AP to become Hyde for the activation turn and up to one further player turn. At the start of that second turn you may revert early. Otherwise revert automatically before your following AP refresh. On reversion, your next player-turn refresh as Jekyll grants 1 fewer AP (normally 2). Early reversion applies the penalty immediately. Clearing a room as Hyde forces reversion and carries any unpaid recovery penalty to the next linked room. Entry into Hyde is unavailable during that recovery turn. Switching does not change the shared abilities or reset their cooldowns or use limits.
02Passive · pick 1

03Active · pick 1
Adrenal Rescue Jekyll only · 1 AP · CD2. A visible, willing, standing ally within four may immediately take a legal move of up to two tiles, spending no AP and skipping walking reactions. Resolve landing hazards, then heal the ally for 2 HP if still standing. The ally can stay in place and receive the heal.
Hurl Aside Hyde only · 1 AP · CD2. Deal 3 damage to an adjacent enemy. If it survives and is standard-weight or lighter, push it up to two tiles away. If a unit blocks the push, deal 1 collision damage to both and stop the push.
04Class passive
Clinical Eye Once per turn, add +1 HP to the first heal on a target below half its healing ceiling, or +1 damage to a Field Formula attack on a target below half maximum HP. The first qualifying action uses the bonus.
05Active · pick 1

Rampage Hyde only · 2 AP · CD2. Hit an adjacent enemy for 4 damage. If that hit defeats it, move up to two legal tiles and strike a different adjacent enemy for 3 damage. Resolve normal movement reactions before the second strike.
06Passive · pick 1
Trauma Ward Once per round as Jekyll, after a squadmate within two tiles takes a direct hit and remains standing below half their healing ceiling, give them two temporary armour until the start of your next turn.
