# Hammer & Ash: Uprising — Class Design Update
**Date:** 22 November 2026  
**Status:** Class identities confirmed; detailed abilities and progression provisional.

## 1. Class Name Changes

The six classes have been renamed and their tactical roles clarified.

| Previous Name | New Name | Tactical Role |
|---|---|---|
| Razor | **Scythe** | Close-quarters damage dealer |
| Sentinel | **Anvil** | Defence and squad protection |
| Observer | **Glaz** | Enemy disruption and battlefield control |
| Marksman | **Needle** | Precision target elimination |
| Engineer | **Iskra** | Technology and environmental manipulation |
| Biochemist | **Alkhimik** | Healing, biological enhancement and chemical warfare |

**Important:** The former Observer's reconnaissance-focused role has been replaced by the Distorter concept. Glaz now specialises in manipulating enemy positioning, movement and actions.

Needle should not necessarily be restricted to ranged weapons. Its role is eliminating priority targets, potentially through sniper rifles, aerial strikes or other powers.

## 2. Revised Class Design Philosophy

Classes are now designed around **the tactical problem they solve**, rather than their weapons, powers or equipment.

Each class will feature multiple distinctive power archetypes, allowing significantly different playstyles within the same tactical role.

Key principles:

- Every class has a recognisable tactical responsibility.
- Each class has access to unique superpowers, mutations and technologies that other classes generally cannot acquire.
- Power archetypes should produce different tactical decisions, not merely different statistics.
- Certain abilities are shared across a class to preserve its common identity.
- Some abilities and specialisation branches are mutually exclusive.
- Hybrid builds may be possible where appropriate, but exclusive powers must retain their distinctive advantages.
- Each archetype must have meaningful weaknesses.
- Skill trees grant abilities and passive improvements, **not items occupying equipment slots**.
- Classes should complement one another and discourage excessively homogeneous squad compositions without introducing unavoidable hard counters.

## 4. Scythe Class Design

**Role:** Close-quarters damage dealer.

Three mutually exclusive power origins:

- **Wolverine:** Armour destruction, regeneration and sustained melee damage. Limited escape options.
- **Arachnid:** Superior conventional mobility, crowd control, dodging and web abilities. Weaker against heavy armour.
- **Blink:** Teleportation, burst damage and repositioning. The most fragile specialisation.

The provisional class tree has a maximum level of 9.

### Key abilities and progression

| Level | Abilities |
|---|---|
| **1** | Shared **Charge**: Rush an enemy and make a close-range attack. **Costs 1 AP.** Choose Wolverine (claws destroy 1 armour per basic attack), Arachnid (Spider Agility, +1 movement), or Blink (Short Blink teleport). |
| **2** | Wolverine: Regrowth (passive HP regeneration). Arachnid: Web Snare (immobilise enemy for one turn). Blink: Phase Strike (bonus damage after teleporting). |
| **3** | Shared **Threatening Presence**: Once per round, melee opportunity attack when an adjacent enemy moves away normally. |
| **4** | Wolverine chooses Plate Ripper (additional armour destruction) or Fast Knit (increased regeneration when ending turn with spare AP). Arachnid chooses Tether Yank (pull enemy two tiles) or Arachnid Sense (next targeted attack misses). Blink chooses Spatial Exchange (swap places with adjacent enemy) or Rift Lunge (teleport around target and attack twice). |
| **5** | Shared choice: Pursuit (move two tiles after close-range kill) or Execution Drill (bonus damage against isolated enemies). |
| **6** | Wolverine/Arachnid: Phase Surge (limited short-range teleport ability). Blink: Rift Relay (teleport an adjacent ally to a cell within six tiles). |
| **7** | Shared **Opening Blow**: First close-range hit each round causes the next allied close-range attack against that target to deal +1 damage. |
| **8** | Wolverine: Rabid Frenzy (heavy double strike and armour reduction). Arachnid: Silkstorm (area webbing and poison for two rounds). Blink: Rift Reaper (teleport, attack isolated target, return to original position). |
| **9** | Shared **Overdrive**: Once per room, temporarily boost movement and close-range damage for one turn. |

### Important Scythe Rules

- Wolverine, Arachnid and Blink origins are mutually exclusive.
- Wolverine's claws and Arachnid's biological powers cannot be acquired by other origins.
- Phase Surge offers limited teleportation to Wolverine and Arachnid without giving them Blink's full teleportation capabilities.
- Blink remains the strongest teleportation specialist.
- Charge costs **1 AP**, superseding the earlier 2 AP version.
- Basic melee opportunity attacks are not universal to every class; Scythe gains them through Threatening Presence.
- Skill-tree powers do not consume equipment slots.

## 5. Development Status and Codex Guidance

**Confirmed design direction:** Six renamed classes, tactical-role-first philosophy, distinct class-exclusive powers, meaningful specialisation trade-offs, and the Observer-to-Distorter role replacement.

**Provisional:** The detailed Scythe progression, ability values, AP costs other than the newly specified Charge cost, cooldowns, numerical effects and balance.

**Not yet designed:** Detailed skill trees for Anvil, Glaz, Needle, Iskra and Alkhimik.

**Implementation instruction:** Treat this as a design update, not evidence that these changes are already implemented. Do not automatically replace working prototype code. Preserve existing functionality and implement the new progression when specifically requested.

Scythe should be the reference model when designing the remaining five classes. Its balance and progression are expected to evolve substantially through playtesting.
